About our wallpaper range

Wallpaper changes the feel of a wall through pattern, colour and texture. At WOW Lifestyle's Ubi showroom, you can explore wallpaper designs and compare how different looks might fit your home. First decide whether you want a feature wall or a pattern across a larger area. Think about the room's purpose, the amount of furniture and decoration already present, and whether a quieter or more noticeable design would suit the space.

Measure the wall carefully and note windows, doors, switches and other interruptions. Look at the wall colour, flooring and nearby furniture before choosing a pattern, since these elements will all be seen together. Consider the scale of the design in relation to the wall: a small repeat and a large motif can create different impressions. Bring a clear photo and measurements when you visit, and ask which product details should be confirmed for your chosen wall.

If you are planning a refresh, compare the designs in person and discuss the room with our team before making a decision. Ask what is currently available to view and what information you may need for your wall. Visit WOW Lifestyle in Singapore to see the wallpaper range at our Ubi showroom. You can make an appointment in advance; appointments are subject to confirmation by our team.
