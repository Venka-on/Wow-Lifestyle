Curtain fabrics and colours: a simple guide

Learn how fabric, colour and light work together in each room.

Think about light first

Sheer fabrics let in soft daylight. Thicker fabrics block more light, which many people prefer in bedrooms. Many homes use both.

Pick a colour that fits the room

Light, neutral colours make a room feel open. Darker colours feel cosy but can make a small room feel smaller. Compare the shade against your wall and furniture.

Measure the window properly

Measure the width of the window and the height from the rail to the floor. Measure twice before you decide.

See the fabric in person

Colour and texture look different in a photo. Visit our showroom to feel the fabric and hold it against the light.
