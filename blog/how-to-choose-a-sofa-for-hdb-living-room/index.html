How to choose a sofa for an HDB living room

Measure your space, pick the right fabric and avoid common buying mistakes.

Start with measurements

Measure the wall where the sofa will sit, then the walkway in front of it. Leave enough space to move around comfortably. Also measure the door, lift and corridor so the sofa can get into your home.

Choose a size that fits the room

A small living room often suits a 2 or 3 seater. Keep the sofa in proportion to the room and the TV console. A sofa that is too big makes the room feel crowded.

Check fabric and comfort in person

Fabric looks different on a screen. Sit on the sofa, check the firmness and feel the fabric. Think about who will use it every day and how easy it is to keep clean.

Match the colour to your home

Neutral colours work with many styles and are easy to refresh with cushions. Bring a photo of your living room when you visit, so our team can help you compare shades.
