About our sound systems range

Sound systems serve different needs, from watching films at home to singing with friends and family. At WOW Lifestyle's Ubi showroom, you can explore home theater and karaoke sound systems and compare the options in person. Begin by deciding what you want the system to do and which room it will be used in. Consider whether it is mainly for television, music or karaoke, and make a note of the equipment you already use.

Room size, furniture arrangement and the position of the television or seating can affect where speakers fit. Measure the available space and note any limits on cables, shelves or floor placement. Check the connections and product details for the equipment you plan to use, and ask about compatibility instead of assuming it will work with every device. If several people will use the system, discuss the controls and setup questions that matter to your household.

Bring a photo or a simple sketch of your room and list the devices you want to connect. Our team can show you the sound systems currently available and help you identify specifications to compare. Visit WOW Lifestyle in Singapore to see the range and discuss your intended setup at the Ubi showroom. Make an appointment in advance; appointments are subject to confirmation by our team.
